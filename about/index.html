Research Experience

  1. Deformable Multi-level Feature Network Applied to Nucleus Segmentation
    Frontiers in Microbiology (SCI Q2, 2024), 3rd author.
    This work focuses on accurately segmenting individual nuclei in H&E-stained pathology images, which is important for digital pathology and cancer diagnosis. We propose DMFNet, which handles features and masks in two stages: deformable convolution with a balanced feature pyramid for multi-scale features, and a one-stage SOLO head that segments instances by location without anchor boxes. On the MoNuSeg 2018 dataset, it reaches 37.8% mAP and 47.4% mAR, outperforming Mask R-CNN, TensorMask, PolarMask, and the SOLO baseline.
